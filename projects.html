Current Projects

Searching for rare earth element biology in geothermal systems

Rare earth elements (REEs) are critical components of modern supply chains and the green energy transition. Microbes may hold the keys to sustainable mining and separation of REEs. We’re searching geothermal systems for microbes, enzymes, and communities that can bioaccumulate these elements.

Microbial aerotrophy: can life survive “on thin air?”

Studying microbial trace gas oxidation and its capacity to sustain life from purely gaseous substrates.

The Microbes of Mt. St. Helens

In 1980, Mt. St. Helens experienced the largest volcanic eruption in United States history. Decades later, we’re figuring out which microbes inhabit its still-active geothermal features, and how they survive on volcanic-derived substrates.
